From smooth waves to loops.

Explore how circular motion and a steady electric-field drift combine to create a trochoid. Adjust the rotation speed and drift speed to reveal the transition through a pointed cycloid.

Physical model

A nonrelativistic particle moves in uniform fields B = B k and E = Ey j + Ez k. This page shows its projection onto the x–y plane.

x(t) = −(A / ωc) cos(ωct) + uty(t) = (A / ωc) sin(ωct)

The shape is set by one ratio

Compare the circular-motion speed A with the magnitude of the drift speed |u|, where u = Ey / B. The boundary A = |u| produces cusps: the particle briefly comes to rest in the plane.

ωc = qB / m > 0   ·   R = A / ωc

Parameters use consistent normalized units. A negative u reverses the drift; the cyclotron frequency is positive in this demonstration.

The three trajectory shapes

Fixed examples: u = 1, ωc = 1 · identical axis scales

(i) Looping trochoid

A = 1.5 > |u|

The particle reverses its horizontal motion and crosses its own path.

(ii) Smooth trochoid

A = 0.5 < |u|

The drift dominates. The particle moves forward while oscillating vertically.

(iii) Cycloid

A = 1.0 = |u|

At each cusp both velocity components vanish. The loop has just collapsed to a point.

Why does the shape change?

Differentiate the position to obtain the velocity:

vx = u + A sin(ωct)vy = A cos(ωct)

For A > |u|, vx changes sign and loops appear. For A < |u|, its sign stays fixed, so there are no loops. When A = |u| > 0, both velocity components vanish together at a cusp.

For positive u, the cusps occur at ωct = 3π/2 + 2πn; for negative u, they occur at π/2 + 2πn.

A moving circle: the center is C(t) = (ut, 0), and the particle stays a distance R = A/ωc from it. The circle rotates while its center drifts at the constant velocity E × B / B².

Special cases: u = 0 gives a circle when A > 0. A = 0 gives a straight line when u ≠ 0. If both vanish, the particle is stationary in this projection.

Along the magnetic field: Ez changes z(t), but leaves this x–y projection unchanged. A three-dimensional trajectory need not be a planar trochoid.

Initial conditions: x(0) = −A/ωc, y(0) = 0, vx(0) = u, and vy(0) = A. The plot uses the exact analytical solution.
